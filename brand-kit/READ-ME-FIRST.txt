PATTERNCHESS - SOCIAL MEDIA KIT
===============================

Hi! This folder has everything you need to make posts that look like
PatternChess: our logo files and our brand colors. Nothing special to
install. You can drag these images straight into Canva, Instagram,
CapCut, or wherever you build posts.

Two questions come up a lot: "what color is that?" and "which logo do I
use?" The answers are below.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------

  READ-ME-FIRST.txt    <- this file
  colors.png           <- a picture of our colors with their codes
  logos/               <- all the logo files (see "WHICH LOGO" below)


OUR COLORS
----------

Open "colors.png" to see them. Most posts only need gold, charcoal, and
off-white.

  Brand gold    #C49B2A    Our main color. The logo and highlights.
  Deep gold     #8B6914    A darker gold that pairs with the brand gold.
  Charcoal      #1A1A1A    Our near-black. Dark backgrounds and text.
  Off-white     #F4F4F0    Our soft white. Light backgrounds.
  Light ink     #E8E8E8    For text or a logo sitting on a dark color.

When a tool (like Canva) asks you to pick a color, type the code and
keep the # sign. For example:  #C49B2A


WHICH LOGO DO I USE?
--------------------

All the logos live in the "logos" folder. Pick the one that matches
what is BEHIND the logo:

  Light or white background   ->   logo-for-light-backgrounds.png
  Dark or photo background    ->   logo-for-dark-backgrounds.png
  Tiny space or a badge       ->   icon-only.png
  Profile picture / avatar    ->   app-icon.png

  social-card-example.png is a finished post you can copy the style from.

These are PNG files, so they work everywhere and have a see-through
(transparent) background. They sit cleanly on top of any design.

Quick check: a light logo on a light background disappears. If you put
the logo down and can't see it, you grabbed the wrong file. Switch to
the other one.


LOGO DO'S AND DON'TS
--------------------

DO:
  - Give the logo room to breathe. Leave some space around it.
  - Keep it gold. The gold is who we are.
  - Keep the word "Chess" in italic. It is part of the look.
  - Resize by dragging a CORNER handle, so the shape stays correct.

PLEASE DON'T:
  - Recolor it (no blue logo, no rainbow, no white-out).
  - Stretch or squish it. Drag the corners, never the sides.
  - Add drop shadows, outlines, or glows.
  - Rotate or tilt it.
  - Put the gold logo on a busy photo where you can't read it.
  - Swap in any chess-knight symbol. Only use the files in here.


FOR A DESIGNER (OPTIONAL)
-------------------------

The "logos/source-files" folder holds the original SVG versions. SVG
files stay razor-sharp at any size, which designers like. If you work
with one, hand them this folder. If an SVG won't open for you, skip that
folder and use the PNGs above. They are all you need.
